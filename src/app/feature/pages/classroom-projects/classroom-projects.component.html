Research and Academic Projects


• Life Cycle Sustainability Assessment of Electric and Hybrid Vehicle Penetration in Tehran
M.Sc. Thesis, Sharif University of Technology | Ongoing
Supervisor: Dr. Akram Avami
  • Developed life cycle environmental and sustainability assessment frameworks for electric and hybrid vehicle deployment scenarios in Tehran.
  • Applied SharifLCA to evaluate environmental impacts across the vehicle life cycle and compare transportation decarbonization pathways.
  • Integrated environmental and economic indicators to support sustainable urban mobility planning.

• Greenhouse Heat Supply Optimization Using Industrial Waste Heat and Thermal Energy Storage
Applied Optimization Course Project
Supervisor: Dr. Moein Moeini-Aghtaie
  • Developed and solved a greenhouse heat supply optimization model using GAMS and Python.

• Life Cycle Assessment and Human Health Impact Analysis of Cement Production
Energy and Environment Course Project
Supervisor: Dr. Akram Avami
  • Conducted LCA of cement production using SharifLCA and modeled emissions from the manufacturing process.
  • Applied AERMOD to estimate air pollutant dispersion and BenMAP to assess PM2.5-related human health impacts.
  • Investigated environmental and public health implications of industrial emissions for sustainable decision-making.
• Energy System Modeling and Decarbonization Pathway Analysis Using OSeMOSYS
Energy Systems Modeling Project, Sharif University of Technology
Supervisor: Dr. Hossein Khajehpour
  • Developed a national energy system model using OSeMOSYS for long-term planning and optimization.
  • Forecasted sectoral electricity demand and analyzed Business-as-Usual and climate mitigation scenarios through 2035.
  • Evaluated renewable energy penetration, carbon pricing, system costs, generation mix evolution, and emissions reduction strategies.
• Mathematical Programming Optimization Solver Development
Advanced Mathematical Programming Project, Sharif University of Technology
Supervisor: Dr. Ramin Roshandel
  • Developed a Python-based linear programming solver implementing the Simplex algorithm from scratch.
  • Solved maximization and minimization problems with equality and inequality constraints.
  • Implemented infeasible, unbounded, degenerate, and multiple-solution case detection with sensitivity analysis.
